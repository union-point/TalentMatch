# TalentMatch

TalentMatch is a private hiring workspace. Recruiters select a job, upload résumés and review the evidence behind each match.

## Visual direction

Use paper white (#fafafa), white panels, graphite text (#191919), secondary ink (#626262), hairline rules (#dedede) and charcoal artwork. Set page and section headings in Instrument Serif. Use Geist for navigation, forms and tables. Small tracked capitals mark sections and metadata. Keep prose in sentence case.

The layered-paper sculpture represents the documents under review. Give it room on the sign-in screen. Inside the workspace, prioritize the job selector, upload queue and candidate table. Use the same folded-sheet mark on the masthead and favicon.

## Screen patterns

- Access screens share a masthead, a single-column form and a photographic panel. At narrow widths, prioritize the form.
- The workspace shares navigation, organization context and one page heading per route. Signed-in users without an organization can still manage account security.
- Place a job's title, company and file information together. Use links for navigation and buttons for mutations.
- Keep scores numerical. Pair statuses with explicit words and distinct icons or tonal fills.
- Keep labels visible, put recovery actions beside errors and preserve entered data after failed requests.
- Keep keyboard focus visible. Let narrow layouts wrap controls and scroll wide data tables locally. Honor reduced motion.
